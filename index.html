Our story

Founded in 2022, on one simple idea.

Dahlias Greenhouse began with a love for creating beautiful, one-of-a-kind arrangements. It has grown into an Atlanta studio designing custom bouquets, weddings, events, statement installations, and our signature mobile Bloom Bar.

Our designs aren't limited to one style. From soft and romantic to bold, colorful, and unexpected, every piece is made to complement the person, event, or experience behind it — with one goal: create something people remember.
